My Prayer For You

May God continue to give you strength.

May He give you many more years of life.

May He give you peace for all the things you've carried.

May He reward you for the love and sacrifices you've given to your family.

And most importantly , may He allow you to see me become the person you've always wanted me to be. I want you to be there when I succeed. I want you to see that all those times you believed in me were worth it.

**

Mum,

I don't think I'll ever fully understand everything you've done for me.

Maybe when I was younger, I didn't see it. Maybe I took some things for granted. Maybe I didn't always make you proud.

But as I grow older, I understand a little more. Every time you kept going, you were carrying something. Every sacrifice meant giving up a part of yourself. The opportunities I have today didn't simply appear. And through all my mistakes, you still chose to believe there was something good ahead of me.

So today, I just want to say thank you.

  • for raising me
  • for believing in me
  • for supporting me
  • for teaching me
  • for being patient with me
  • for simply being my Mum

I don't know exactly where life will take me, but I know one thing. I want you to be there when I make it. I want you to see me succeed. I want you to look back one day and know that everything you did for me mattered.

"Mum, you never gave up on me… and I'm glad you didn't."

Until then, I'll keep trying to become someone you can be proud of.

Happy Birthday, Mum.

May God bless you with many more years, more peace, more happiness, and more beautiful moments with the people you love.

I love you, Mum.

.... Your son
